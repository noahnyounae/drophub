About

DropHub is a small self-hosted tool for exchanging files through single-use links. Its source code is published at github.com/noahnyounae/drophub.

Using a link

  • Each upload link accepts one file, once. Each download link works once.
  • Links may expire or be revoked by the person who created them at any time.
  • Only upload files you have the right to share. Do not upload unlawful or malicious content.
  • A link may be protected by a password; do not share it with anyone else.

Data we keep

For each transfer the server stores the file, its name, size and SHA-256 checksum, the date, the IP address of the sender, and the name and message you optionally provide. The owner of the instance can read, download and delete this data. Files are not scanned or shared with third parties by the software. Traffic may transit through Cloudflare when a tunnel is used.

No warranty

The software and this service are provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. The checksum shown after a transfer confirms the file arrived intact; it does not vouch for the content of the file.
